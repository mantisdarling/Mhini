Media quality audit findings, 2026-08-22

Source inventory covered 17 unique live media URLs: still images, five mobile derivatives, two MP4 videos, poster frames, and the mark. Every audited CDN source returned HTTP 200 with the expected image or video content type.

The two videos are H.264, yuv420p, 736x414, with durations of 31.85 and 33.32 seconds. Their source dimensions are retained because no higher-resolution original is available in the retained source set. The hero remains autoplaying and the closing scene now uses a responsive mobile poster with metadata preload so phones do not eagerly fetch the full closing video.

Phone and tablet full-page captures show the image-led chapters, mobile crops, project carousel, evidence, Stack, closing motion, and contact section are present. Seven desktop stills were replaced with quality-preserving public WebP variants, and the brand mark was reduced from a 1920px PNG to a 512px alpha-safe WebP. The existing mobile derivatives remain separate and responsive.
